Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026 · Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Desh Paribartan Media Facebook application (Meta App ID 1254518222861809), operated by the Desh Paribartan Media team, handles information when it interacts with the Facebook Page Desh Paribartan Media.

1. What the application does

The application is an automated news-publishing tool. It collects publicly available news headlines and articles from Nepali news sources (RSS feeds), prepares text and image posts, and publishes them to our own Facebook Page. It also reads engagement statistics (views, reactions, comments) for our own Page so we can improve our coverage schedule.

2. Facebook permissions the application uses

  • pages_show_list — to identify our own Page.
  • pages_manage_posts — to publish posts, photos and reels to our own Page.
  • pages_read_engagement — to read engagement metrics of our own Page.

3. What information is involved

  • Page data: content we publish ourselves and aggregate engagement statistics of our own Page.
  • News content: publicly available news headlines and article text from public RSS feeds, used to prepare posts.

The application does not collect, store, or share personal data of Facebook users. It does not access your friends list, your timeline, your messages, or any other personal profile information. It operates only on the Page listed above.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or transfer any information to third parties. Data is processed only through the Meta Graph API for the purposes described above. This policy page itself is hosted as a static page on GitHub Pages and contains no tracking scripts or cookies.

5. Data retention

Published posts remain on our Facebook Page unless removed. Internally kept article summaries and engagement statistics are retained only as long as needed for the operation of the news service, and are deleted afterwards.
